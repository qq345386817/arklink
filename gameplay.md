# How decisions work in ArkLink

AVAILABLE NOW · VERSION 1.2.0

ArkLink is a branching visual novel and fictional climate mystery, not a real-world insurance service. Read a disaster claim, compare evidence and choose between corporate orders, a community’s survival and your family’s medical access. Choices leave traces in later scenes and recorded routes.

The opening case starts with a 90-second action budget and two override permits, not a real-time reading countdown. Reading, opening logs, saving and resuming do not spend it. Version 1.2.0 also refreshes the reader, history and chapter navigation. Older first-chapter saves keep their original story mode until you replay the chapter.

## The action budget is not a reading timer

A new first-chapter run starts with a 90-second action budget. Reading dialogue, checking the log, saving and resuming do not spend it. Charged operations show their cost before you choose. This is not the length of the story.

## Override permits create a trade-off

You have two permits in the first chapter. Some evidence copies and emergency actions consume one. Saving both copies can leave no permit for freezing denial later; routine actions remain available.

## Compare evidence before choosing

The T-402 cross-check places 179 days, a 180-day threshold and a 2.00mm reading beside a write timestamp eleven minutes ahead of the field upload. You choose whether to lock the timestamp, request a physical recheck or buy a review window.

## A profile shapes consequences, not a ready-made answer

Human-first, system-correction and evidence-leverage profiles influence the effects of your decisions. The released game shows operation costs, not advance score changes or route predictions on every choice. Read the evidence and discover what your decision leaves behind.

## The window can survive, freeze or expire

The first chapter’s receipt records time spent and permits left. A freeze or an expired review window is acknowledged in chapter two. Resource rules apply to this opening case, not every chapter.

## Sound is optional

Terminal sound effects default to off. An independent switch enables selection, message, authorisation, denial, archive-unlock and low-window cues. Reading remains possible with sound off.

## Follow routes and unlock archives

Progress through main chapters and discover side-story archives and different endings. Route records let you revisit the traces of completed decisions. No fixed playtime or ending count is promised here.

## Read at your own pace

Version 1.2.0 refreshes the reader and history layout. Scroll long messages or use the reader’s previous and next controls, review the log and return to the story. Reading and navigation do not spend the T-402 action budget.

## Save and replay

Progress, operation costs and pending choices save locally. Restart Chapter lets you explore another route while keeping discovered files and recorded outcomes; it is different from clearing all progress. Older chapter-one saves keep their original story mode until replayed. Saves do not promise cloud sync or cross-device transfer.
