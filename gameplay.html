How decisions work in ArkLink

ArkLink is a story-led visual novel, not a real-world insurance service. You read the case, choose an action and follow the consequences. The T-402 mechanics below are available in version 1.1.0, alongside branching story play and local progress.

AVAILABLE NOW · VERSION 1.1.0

Version 1.1.0 includes an action budget, limited override permits, evidence cross-checks, persistent profile abilities and optional terminal sound effects. Start a new first-chapter run to use the T-402 operating window; older saves keep their original story mode until you replay the chapter.

The decision budget is not a reading timer

A new first-chapter run starts with 90 seconds of action time. Reading dialogue, checking the log and resuming a save do not spend it. Operations show their cost before you choose.

Override permits create a trade-off

You have two permits in the first chapter. Some evidence copies and emergency actions consume one. Saving both copies can leave no permit for freezing denial later; routine actions remain available.

Compare evidence before choosing

The T-402 cross-check places 179 days, a 180-day threshold and a 2.00mm reading beside a write timestamp eleven minutes ahead of the field upload. You choose whether to lock the timestamp, request a physical recheck or buy a review window.

A profile changes the effect of your choices

Human-first choices reduce public anger by an extra 2 when they already reduce it. System-correction choices add 2 company trust when they raise it. Evidence-leverage choices add 2 evidence strength when they raise it, subject to stat limits.

The window can survive, freeze or expire

The first chapter’s receipt records time spent and permits left. A freeze or an expired review window is acknowledged in chapter two. Resource rules apply to this opening case, not every chapter.

Sound is optional

Terminal sound effects default to off. An independent switch enables selection, message, authorisation, denial, archive-unlock and low-window cues. Reading remains possible with sound off.

Save and replay

The game preserves action costs and the pending destination if play is interrupted. Older chapter-one saves keep their original story mode; replaying enables the operating window.
