# Frequently asked questions

## What kind of game is ArkLink?

ArkLink: Global Extremes is a branching visual novel and interactive fiction game with a fictional climate mystery. As catastrophe claims actuary Carter Li, investigate altered evidence and make difficult choices about a valley’s survival, your son’s medical access and corporate orders.

## Can I play offline?

Yes. After installation, the bundled story and local saves work offline. Downloading, updating and opening external support or store links require a connection.

## Is ArkLink free, and does it have ads?

ArkLink is free to play, with no ads. No game account is required; story progress is stored on your device. The download link opens the official App Store listing.

## Which platforms and languages are supported?

The official download linked here is for iPhone and iPad, with iOS or iPadOS 15.1 or later. English and Simplified Chinese are included. No Google Play download is listed on this site.

## Does reading spend the 90-second budget?

No. T-402 uses an action budget. It decreases only when you choose a charged operation. Taking time to read is free.

## What happens when override permits run out?

In the first chapter, costly emergency actions become unavailable with a reason shown. Standard sign-off remains available, so running out of permits does not trap the story.

## What is available in version 1.2.0?

Version 1.2.0 refreshes reading, history and chapter navigation. It also includes the T-402 action budget, override permits, profile abilities, evidence cross-checks and optional terminal sounds. With an older first-chapter save, replay the chapter to enable the action window.

## Can I explore different routes and endings?

Yes. Follow the main chapters, unlock side-story archives and discover different endings. Restart a chapter to try another choice; discovered files and recorded outcomes remain available. The site does not promise a particular ending count or total playtime.

## Can I transfer my save to another device?

Progress is stored locally. Cloud sync and cross-device transfer are not promised. Restart Chapter replays a chapter; Clear Progress deletes the local story record.

## Do choices show the result before I decide?

Charged operations show resource costs and unavailable actions explain why they are blocked. Release builds hide developer score-change and route previews. Discover the narrative consequences after making your decision.

## Are these real climate or insurance recommendations?

No. The setting and claims are fictional game content. They are not professional climate, medical, financial or insurance advice.
