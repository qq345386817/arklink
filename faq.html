Frequently asked questions

What kind of game is ArkLink?

ArkLink: Global Extremes is an interactive climate thriller visual novel. You play Carter Li, a catastrophe claims actuary, and make decisions about evidence, treatment access and corporate orders.

Can I play offline?

Yes. After installation, the bundled story and local saves work offline. Downloading, updating and opening external support or store links require a connection.

Do I need an account?

No account is required to play the story. Progress is saved on your device.

Which platforms and languages are supported?

The public download linked here is the iPhone and iPad App Store app. It supports English and Simplified Chinese. This website does not advertise a Google Play release.

Is the game paid?

Check the price on the App Store in your region. Prices and promotions may change; the website does not promise a fixed worldwide price.

Does reading spend the 90-second budget?

No. In the upcoming T-402 upgrade, this is an action budget. It decreases only when you choose a charged operation. Taking time to read is free.

What happens when override permits run out?

In the upcoming first-chapter mechanics, costly emergency actions become unavailable with a reason shown. Standard sign-off remains available, so running out of permits does not trap the story.

Are the new mechanics already in the App Store app?

The operating window, profile abilities, evidence cross-checks and terminal audio described in the update preview are in development. They may not be present in the version currently available to download.

Can I replay a route or transfer a save?

You can restart a chapter to explore another route. Saves are local; this website does not promise cloud sync or transfer between devices. Clearing progress removes the local story record.

Are these real climate or insurance recommendations?

No. The setting and claims are fictional game content. They are not professional climate, medical, financial or insurance advice.
