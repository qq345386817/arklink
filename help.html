Help

Start a new run

Open ArkLink and tap Start Survival Review. The app will begin with a short permission calibration before Chapter 1.

Continue progress

Use Continue Current Run from the home screen or Chapter Menu. Progress is stored locally on the device.

Change language

ArkLink follows the device language when available and currently supports English and Simplified Chinese.

Reset progress

Use Restart or Clear Progress inside the app if you want to replay from the beginning.

Offline play

After installation, the story and local save system work without an account.
