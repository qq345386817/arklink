Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 26, 2026

Overview

ArkLink is a standalone visual novel. It does not require an account to play.

Personal information

The app does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location, or account credentials.

Local data

Story progress, route records, settings, and language preferences may be stored locally on your device.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, they are used only for local in-game reminders and can be disabled in system settings.

Analytics and tracking

ArkLink does not use third-party advertising trackers and does not track you across apps or websites.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to cameoshell09@gmail.com.
