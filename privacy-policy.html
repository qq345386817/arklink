Privacy policy

Policy updated: October 11, 2026

Overview

ArkLink is a standalone visual novel. It does not require a game account to play.

Personal information

The app does not ask for your name, contacts, photos, precise location or account credentials. Contacting support by email shares the information you choose to include with the developer.

Local data

Story progress, route records and settings may be stored locally on your device. The upcoming sound setting and action budget are also saved locally.

Notifications

If enabled, notifications are local in-game reminders. You can disable them in system settings.

Analytics and tracking

ArkLink does not use third-party advertising trackers or track you across apps and websites.

Microphone

The upcoming terminal sound effects are playback only and do not require microphone access.
